FAQ

Straight answers

Who is this for?

B2B companies with a clear buyer, a sales call that can close, and someone who can take those calls. If you grow from referrals and inbound today and want a system that brings in people who have never heard of you, this is built for that.

What does “qualified” mean?

A booked call with a contact who matches the ICP we agree on up front — company type, title, and fit. If they are not a fit, it is not a qualified call. You do not pay for it.

What do you do vs. what do I still do?

We build the list, write copy, send, handle replies, and book. You approve ICP and positioning at the start, then show up to qualified calls and sell. You do not run the outreach.

How does pricing work?

There is a system fee of about $600/month for infrastructure (domains, inboxes, tooling). On top of that, you pay per qualified call that is booked and shows. You do not pay per send, per reply, or for no-shows.
